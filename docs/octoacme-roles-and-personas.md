# OctoAcme Personas

This document defines typical roles and responsibilities used in OctoAcme project docs and exercises.

---

## Developers

### Role Summary
Developers design, build, test, and deliver software components. They collaborate with product and project leads to implement features that meet acceptance criteria and quality standards.

### Responsibilities
- Implement features and fixes to meet acceptance criteria
- Write and maintain tests and documentation
- Participate in design and code reviews
- Assist in estimating and planning work
- Help identify technical risks and propose mitigations

### Goals
- Deliver reliable, maintainable code
- Reduce cycle time from idea to production
- Maintain high test coverage and observability

### Typical Communication
- Daily standups and sprint planning
- PR descriptions and code review comments
- Technical design docs when needed

---

## Product Managers

### Role Summary
Product Managers define what should be built to deliver customer and business value. They own the product vision, prioritize the backlog, and measure outcomes.

### Responsibilities
- Define problem statements and success metrics
- Prioritize the roadmap and backlog
- Collaborate with stakeholders and engineering on trade-offs
- Validate solutions through user research and metrics

### Goals
- Maximize customer value and impact
- Make clear, data-driven prioritization decisions
- Ensure product-market fit and usability

### Typical Communication
- Weekly alignment with PM and engineering leads
- Roadmap updates and stakeholder briefings
- Acceptance criteria and feature specs

---

## Project Managers

### Role Summary
Project Managers coordinate delivery activities, manage schedules, risks, and communications. They enable the team to deliver on commitments efficiently.

### Responsibilities
- Create and maintain project plans and timelines
- Manage risks, dependencies, and resource constraints
- Facilitate meetings (kickoff, planning, retrospectives)
- Ensure consistent project documentation and status reporting
- Coordinate cross-team and stakeholder communication

### Goals
- Deliver projects on time and within scope
- Minimize unplanned work and escalations
- Maintain transparency and alignment across stakeholders

### Typical Communication
- Weekly status updates and stakeholder reports
- Risk registers and decision logs
- Coordination via project boards and meeting facilitation

---

## Engineering Leads

### Role Summary
Engineering Leads provide technical direction and delivery leadership across a team or workstream. They align implementation choices with product goals, team capacity, and quality expectations.

### Responsibilities
- Own technical execution for a feature, subsystem, or initiative
- Coordinate architecture and implementation decisions with developers and stakeholders
- Help prioritize engineering work and resolve delivery trade-offs
- Support team health, mentoring, and technical decision-making
- Partner with Product Managers and Project Managers on planning, sequencing, and risk management

### Goals
- Deliver technically sound solutions on time
- Maintain team alignment on architecture and quality standards
- Reduce delivery friction across dependencies and handoffs

### Typical Communication
- Engineering planning and roadmap reviews
- Technical design discussions and architecture reviews
- Coordination with PMs and Product Managers on sequencing and trade-offs

### Interaction with Existing Roles
- Works with Developers to guide implementation and technical quality
- Collaborates with Product Managers to translate priorities into actionable engineering work
- Supports Project Managers by clarifying delivery dependencies, risks, and milestones

---

## QA Leads / Test Managers

### Role Summary
QA Leads and Test Managers define quality expectations, validation strategies, and release readiness criteria for project work.

### Responsibilities
- Develop test plans and quality gates for features and releases
- Define acceptance criteria validation and regression coverage needs
- Coordinate manual and automated testing efforts with engineering teams
- Track defects, test status, and release readiness signals
- Partner with Product Managers and stakeholders on user experience and quality expectations

### Goals
- Reduce quality risk before release
- Ensure that customer-facing functionality is validated against business intent
- Support predictable, reliable delivery outcomes

### Typical Communication
- QA planning sessions and bug triage
- Release readiness reviews and go/no-go checkpoints
- Status reporting to PMs, Product Managers, and stakeholders

### Interaction with Existing Roles
- Works closely with Developers to validate features and identify defects early
- Aligns with Product Managers on acceptance criteria and quality standards
- Provides Project Managers with release-readiness risk information and dependency visibility

---

## Security / Compliance Leads

### Role Summary
Security and Compliance Leads ensure project work follows required controls, standards, and risk policies. They protect the organization and customers by identifying vulnerabilities and governance gaps early.

### Responsibilities
- Review architecture, data handling, and implementation decisions for security and compliance risks
- Define required controls, review checkpoints, and approval standards
- Coordinate security testing, vulnerability assessments, and mitigation planning
- Support incident response readiness and escalation paths when needed
- Partner with Product and Delivery teams on risk trade-offs and mitigation strategies

### Goals
- Reduce security and compliance risk in project delivery
- Ensure the solution meets internal and external obligations
- Build trust in production readiness and customer safety

### Typical Communication
- Security reviews and design checkpoints
- Risk and compliance briefings with leadership and stakeholders
- Escalation updates during incidents or high-risk changes

### Interaction with Existing Roles
- Reviews work with Developers and Engineering Leads to reduce technical risk
- Advises Project Managers and Product Managers on risk, impact, and governance constraints
- Coordinates with stakeholders when release approvals or policy exceptions are required

---

## Technical Architects / Solution Architects

### Role Summary
Technical Architects shape the system design and integration approach to ensure solutions are scalable, maintainable, and aligned with organizational standards.

### Responsibilities
- Define architecture principles, patterns, and design guardrails
- Review integrations, dependencies, and platform choices across systems
- Help resolve complex technical trade-offs and cross-team design questions
- Support planning for scalability, operability, and long-term maintainability
- Partner with Engineering Leads to translate strategy into delivery-friendly design decisions

### Goals
- Build coherent, scalable, and sustainable solutions
- Reduce rework caused by architectural drift or unclear integration patterns
- Support long-term platform consistency across projects

### Typical Communication
- Architecture reviews and design workshops
- Cross-team technical alignment and dependency planning
- Documentation of standards, patterns, and integration guidance

### Interaction with Existing Roles
- Guides Developers and Engineering Leads on design direction and technical trade-offs
- Collaborates with Product Managers on feasibility and roadmap impacts
- Works with Project Managers to identify architectural dependencies and risk exposure

---

## Customer Success / Enablement Partners

### Role Summary
Customer Success and Enablement Partners connect delivery work to customer outcomes, adoption, and stakeholder readiness. They help ensure that solved problems actually translate into user value and smooth rollouts.

### Responsibilities
- Validate that project outputs meet customer and stakeholder needs
- Coordinate readiness activities such as training, communications, and adoption support
- Surface customer pain points, feedback, and operational realities for prioritization
- Help shape release communication and support planning
- Partner with Product and Project teams to ensure a successful rollout experience

### Goals
- Improve adoption, usability, and customer satisfaction
- Minimize launch friction and support burden
- Keep customer needs visible throughout delivery

### Typical Communication
- Customer feedback loops and stakeholder check-ins
- Release readiness reviews and enablement planning
- Cross-functional updates with PMs and Product Managers

### Interaction with Existing Roles
- Provides Product Managers with customer insight and success indicators
- Helps Project Managers align launch planning and stakeholder communication
- Works with Developers and QA on usability and readiness validation

---

## Release Managers / Deployment Coordinators

### Role Summary
Release Managers or Deployment Coordinators plan and coordinate the execution of releases to ensure that changes are shipped in a controlled and observable way.

### Responsibilities
- Schedule deployment windows and align release sequencing
- Coordinate rollback, contingency, and incident readiness planning
- Support rollout communications for project teams and stakeholders
- Verify post-deployment health checks and key success signals
- Partner with QA, Security, and Engineering Leads on release readiness

### Goals
- Reduce release risk and operational disruption
- Increase confidence in deployment quality and stakeholder communication
- Improve the consistency of rollout practices across projects

### Typical Communication
- Release planning and go/no-go reviews
- Deployment check-ins, incident updates, and post-release reports
- Coordination with support, stakeholders, and delivery teams

### Interaction with Existing Roles
- Works with Engineering Leads and QA Leads to confirm deployment readiness
- Coordinates with Project Managers on timing, risk communication, and stakeholder updates
- Helps Product Managers and Customer Success teams prepare for launch impact and support needs

---

## How these personas are used in the exercise
- Use these persona definitions to frame scenarios and sample interactions in the Skills Exercise.
- Each persona can be used as a persona prompt for Copilot Spaces to shape role-specific guidance.
- These roles are intended to clarify accountability, improve handoffs, and support consistent delivery across product, engineering, quality, security, and stakeholder teams.
